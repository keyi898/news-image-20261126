A single 1024×1024 image summing up the world's news reported on
Thursday 26 November 2026, drawn from scratch with Python and Pillow
(flat vector-style pictograms, no photographs, no copied artwork).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) copies the
bundled `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`) after a size and PNG-header check.
Both runs write identical bytes.

4. **Huawei verdict** — a US jury convicted Huawei on 10 of 12

   acquitted it of racketeering (Reuters / CNN, 24 Nov 2026).
5. **Canary Islands record** — nearly 2,000 migrants arrived in
   24 hours on 15 boats, a single-day record (AP / Reuters,
   25 Nov 2026).
6. **South Africa** — the Western Cape High Court declared the
   2022 Phala Phala panel report unlawful, ending the impeachment
   path against President Ramaphosa for now (Al Jazeera, 25 Nov 2026).
7. **UN femicide brief** — 47,000 women and girls were killed by
